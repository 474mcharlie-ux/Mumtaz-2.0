Flexible Purchase Terms

Five ways to drive it home

Whether you're paying cash or financing through a SACCO, we guide you through the paperwork so the process stays simple.

Cash Purchase

Best-price guarantee with same day processing and delivery one of the fastest ways to own a vehicle.

Hire Purchase

A deposit of 50%–70%, with the balance paid over 12–24 months. No bank statement or CRB clearance required.

Bank / SACCO Financing

We guide you through financing with your bank or institution. Commitment fees are paid at our office only, after you receive the proforma invoice.

Trade-In Option

Our technical team evaluates your current vehicle. Once approved, any price difference is settled by cash or hire purchase.

Imports

Competitive rates on quality imported vehicles, with full support through the importation process from selection to delivery.
